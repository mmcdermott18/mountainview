Televisits

As medical care and technology have evolved, we are now able to offer video televisits to those patients whose condition does not allow for them to come to the office in person. In this case, you can stay in the comfort of your home, and still “see” your provider. Televisits are covered by many/most insurance policies. They are accomplished through an application called Doximity.

In order to participate in a televisit, you will need a smartphone. The process is this:

  1. Right before your visit, you’ll get a phone call from our staff to get information for the visit, such as vital signs, current medications, chief complaints, etc. She will confirm that you have received the text. She may also collect your copay.
  2. At time of visit, click on the link in that text to join the call.
  3. Your provider will able to join the call, much like a “Facetime” call.
Important Information About Televisits
  • VA law requires that you be physically in the Commonwealth of Virginia at the time of your televisit. You will be required to confirm this before the provider will sign on for your visit.
  • Televisits will be billed to insurance and/or you will be billed just as if you came into the office. Any amount not covered by insurance will be your responsibility. Copays are due at the time of the visit, and can be paid over the phone with the nursing staff who calls you.
  • Televisits are scheduled just as an in-person visit to our office; patients are provided with the same staff and provider time as an office visit. Televisits are not provided after hours.
  • *Televisits are not an option for new patients, who must be seen in the office.
  • Televisits are not an option for physicals or Well-Child checks. Medicare Annual Wellness visits CAN be provided by Televisit, as these are not actual physical exams.
